# 🕸️ The "Catch All" Campaign Reconstruction Strategy
**Date:** July 3, 2026  
**Target Campaign:** `Catch All | PMax | Medium Margin` (Transition to Standard Shopping Catch-All)

---

## 🎯 1. Why Transitioning to a Standard Shopping "Catch-All" is a Mastermove

A "Catch-All" campaign is designed to act as a safety net. It captures searches for the thousands of low-volume, niche products in your catalog that do not have their own dedicated, high-budget campaigns. 

Using **Performance Max (PMax)** for a Catch-All is highly dangerous and inefficient. Here is why transitioning to **Standard Shopping** is a massive upgrade:

| Risk with PMax Catch-All | Solution with Standard Shopping Catch-All |
| :--- | :--- |
| **High CPCs & Budget Waste:** PMax will display ads on YouTube, Gmail, and Display networks, matching to completely broad, non-commercial search terms (like video games or toddler alphabet videos) to spend its budget. | **100% Core Shopping Traffic:** Standard Shopping *only* displays ads in Google's main Shopping grid and search results. It completely blocks display and video network waste. |
| **Asset Cannibalization:** PMax generates automatically-created text and image ads. This means your Catch-All PMax campaign will bid on keywords that belong in your dedicated `BL00` and `CL00` campaigns, stealing their traffic. | **Purity of Intent:** Standard Shopping has no text assets. It matches searchers *strictly* to the title, description, and price of actual products in your feed. |
| **Overbidding Niche Products:** PMax’s automated bidding is aggressive and can bid $3.00 to $5.00+ to win an auction for a small, niche $5.00 part or accessory, wiping out your margins. | **Low-Cost Bid Control:** You can set a manual, low maximum CPC (e.g., $0.40 to $0.60) or a high Target ROAS (300%+). This guarantees you only win auctions when clicks are extremely cheap and profitable. |

## 🛠️ 2. Step-by-Step "Catch-All" Shopping Campaign Setup

### Campaign Name: `Catch All | Shopping | Low Bid | Safety Net`

### Step 1: Core Configuration
1. Click the blue **"+"** to create a new campaign.
2. Select **Create a campaign without a goal's guidance**.
3. Select **Shopping** as your campaign type.
4. Select your linked Google Merchant Center account.
5. Select **Standard Shopping campaign** (do **NOT** select Performance Max).

### Step 2: Campaign Priority & Bidding (The Safety Net Logic)
This is where we configure the campaign to behave strictly as a "catch-all" that yields high profitability:

1. **Campaign Priority:** Select **Low** (This is crucial! It tells Google: *"If another campaign targets this product ID, let them bid first. Only let this campaign bid if no other campaign is active."*)
2. **Bidding Strategy:** 
   * **Option A (Highly Recommended):** Select **Manual CPC** and **UNCHECK "Enable Enhanced CPC"**. 
     * *Set your default bid to:* **`$0.45`**. 
     * *Why:* This hard-caps your spend. Google can never charge you more than $0.45 per click. It forces Google to find cheap, highly-profitable, high-intent traffic across your entire long-tail product line.
   * **Option B:** Select **Target ROAS** and set a high target of **`350%`** or **`400%`**.
3. **Daily Budget:** Set to **$30.00/day** or **$40.00/day** (since you are bidding so low, this budget will go a very long way!).

### Step 3: Networks & Locations
1. **Networks:** 🛑 **UNCHECK "Include Google search partners"**.
2. **Locations:** United States (with **Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations**).

### Step 4: Listing Group Configuration (Excluding Dedicated Products)
To prevent your Catch-All campaign from showing your `BL00` (Building Letters), `VL07` (Truck Lettering), or `CL00` (Marquee Letters) products, we must exclude them:

1. Go to the campaign's **Listing Groups** tab.
2. Click the edit pencil next to **"All products"**.
3. Subdivide by **Category** or **Custom Label 0**.
4. Check the boxes next to **`BL00`**, **`VL07`**, and **`CL00`** (and any other dedicated campaign product categories you create).
5. Click **Save**.
6. Back on the Listing Groups screen:
   * **`BL00`** -> Change status to **EXCLUDED**
   * **`VL07`** -> Change status to **EXCLUDED**
   * **`CL00`** -> Change status to **EXCLUDED**
   * **`Everything else in All products`** -> Leave as **Submitted / Active**.

*By doing this, your Catch-All campaign acts as a perfect shield—covering 100% of your remaining long-tail product catalog (such as yard signs, sidewalk frames, or custom banners) at a highly profitable, low CPC, while leaving your specialized campaigns completely free to win their high-intent auctions!*
