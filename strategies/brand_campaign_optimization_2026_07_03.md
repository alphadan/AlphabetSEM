# 🏷️ Brand Campaign Optimization Strategy
**Date:** July 3, 2026  
**Target Campaign:** `Branded Search | Alphabet Signs`

## 📊 1. Forensic Diagnosis of Your Brand Campaign

Your Brand campaign is doing exceptionally well overall—converting at **$9.21 per acquisition (CPA)** and driving **68 conversions** per month. 

However, your screenshots reveal **two critical areas of inefficiency and restriction** that, when fixed, will instantly lower your cost and protect your brand name from competitors.

### Issue A: Google’s Loose "Exact Match Close Variants" Trap
In your search terms report, we discovered that Google is matching highly expensive, **generic category terms** to your exact brand keywords because both contain the words "signs" or "letters":
* **`sign letters`** *(Matched as an Exact Match Close Variant to your brand)* - Cost you **$55.57 for 25 clicks with 0 conversions** ($2.22 per click).
* **`sign lettering`** *(Matched as an Exact Match Close Variant)* - Cost you **$14.88 for 6 clicks with 0 conversions**.
* **`letters for sign board`** *(Matched as an Exact Match Close Variant)* - Cost you **$4.80 for 2 clicks with 0 conversions**.
* **Why this is a problem:** Your brand budget should only be spent on users who explicitly search for your business by name (`"Alphabet Signs"`). Generic queries belong in your newly created, optimized Search and Shopping campaigns. Letting Google treat `"sign letters"` as a close variant of your company name is a direct waste of your brand budget.

### Issue B: "Bid setting limited by maximum bid limit" Warning
* **The Cause:** This campaign utilizes a bid strategy (likely **Target Impression Share** to capture 95%+ of your own brand queries) with a **Maximum CPC Bid Limit** that is set too low (e.g., $1.50 or $2.00).
* **The Danger:** If competitors (like national readerboard suppliers, Uline, or Etsy) bid on your brand name `"alphabet signs"` to steal your customers, Google wants to raise your bid slightly to guarantee you keep the **Absolute Top Spot** (the #1 organic-looking ad at the very top of Google). Because of your strict bid cap, Google cannot raise your bid, allowing competitors to sometimes win your brand auctions.

---

## 🛠️ 2. The Brand Optimization Action Plan

To resolve these issues immediately, complete these two steps:

### Step 1: Add Exact Negatives for Generic Leakage
Add these exact terms as **Negative Keywords** *inside* your `Branded Search | Alphabet Signs` campaign. This tells Google: *"Do NOT match generic sign-letter searchers to my cheap brand campaign."*

```text
-[sign letters]
-[sign lettering]
-[letters for sign board]
-[raised lettering]
-[3d letters]
```

### Step 2: Resolve the "Bid Setting Limited" Warning
1. Go to your **Campaign Settings** for `Branded Search | Alphabet Signs`.
2. Expand the **Bidding** section.
3. If you are using **Target Impression Share** (e.g., targeting *95% of Absolute Top of Page*):
   * Locate your **Maximum CPC bid limit**.
   * Increase this cap to **`$3.50`** or **`$4.00`**.
   * **🛑 Do not worry:** Google will **not** actually charge you $4.00 for your own brand clicks. Your brand Quality Score is a perfect 10/10, so your actual average brand CPC will remain extremely cheap (around $0.95 as seen on your screen). This higher cap simply gives Google's bidding engine the "breathing room" to bid aggressively and block competitors whenever they try to piggyback on your brand name.
