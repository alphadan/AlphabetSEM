## 🛠️ 3. Resolving Technical and Setup Warnings

Your Search campaign screenshot displays three warning boxes. Here is how to fix each one:

### Warning 1: "Enhanced conversions has setup issues impacting performance"
* **The Issue:** Enhanced Conversions hashes and sends first-party user data (like email and phone number) securely to Google during checkout. If this is broken, Google is missing conversion matches, making your Smart Bidding algorithm partially blind.
* **The Fix:** 
  1. Go to your Google Ads panel -> **Tools and Settings** -> **Measurement: Conversions**.
  2. Click on your primary purchase conversion action.
  3. Expand the **Enhanced Conversions** tab.
  4. Ensure it is turned ON and check your implementation method (usually Google Tag Manager or the Google Tag).
  5. If you use GTM, ensure that your checkout data layer is correctly capturing and formatting the `hashed_email` and `hashed_phone_number` variables.

### Warning 2: "Missing callout extensions / assets"
* **The Issue:** Your ad is physically smaller on the page because it is missing assets. This lowers your Quality Score and raises your average cost-per-click (CPC).
* **The Fix:** Add these assets directly to the `CL00 | Search` campaign:
  * **Sitelink Assets:**
    * Link 1: *Wagner Zip-Change Letters* (Directs to the Wagner category)
    * Link 2: *Gemini Pronto Letters* (Directs to the Pronto category)
    * Link 3: *Sign Letter Accessories* (Directs to tracks/squeegees)
  * **Callout Assets:**
    * `"Ships in 24 Hours"`
    * `"Lifetime Warranty"`
    * `"Authorized Gemini Dealer"`
    * `"B2B fleet pricing"`
  * **Structured Snippet Assets:**
    * Select Header: **Brands** -> `Gemini, Wagner, Pronto, Zip-Change`

### Warning 3: "Smart Bidding limited by target"
* **The Issue:** Your Target ROAS is set to 250%, but because the campaign only has 2.65 conversions in the past week, the algorithm doesn't have enough data to comfortably bid, so it is bidding extremely conservatively.
* **The Fix:** Once you harvest the high-intent keywords from PMax and add Sitelinks/Callouts, your weekly conversion volume will rise. Once you hit **15+ conversions per month**, the bidding warning will automatically disappear, and Google will confidently scale your delivery.
