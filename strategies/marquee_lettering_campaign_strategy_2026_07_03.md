## 💡 2. Audience & Search Themes in Standard Campaigns

You asked: **"When I create new campaigns do I need to add any audience or search terms or is that only for PMax?"**

### **The Answer: NO. Search Themes and Audience Signals are ONLY used in PMax.**

Here is the fundamental difference in how targeting works between PMax and Standard campaigns:

| Setting | Performance Max (PMax) | Standard Search & Shopping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search Themes** | **Required / Crucial:** Acts as "hints" for Google's machine learning to find broad-match audiences across YouTube, Gmail, Display, and Search. | **Do Not Use:** Standard Search relies on **explicit keywords** with strict match types (Phrase `""` and Exact `[]`) to control auctions. |
| **Audience Signals** | **Required / Crucial:** Used as lookalike "hints" to find similar users on consumer feeds (like YouTube or Gmail). | **Optional (Observation Mode Only):** You do not target audiences directly. Instead, you add them as "Observation" layers. Google simply monitors how those audiences behave, allowing you to add positive or negative bid adjustments later. |

### The Standard Shopping Rule:
When you launch a Standard Shopping campaign, **the product feed is your targeting**. Google reads your product titles, categories, and descriptions to match ads to transactional queries in the Google Shopping tab. You do not need to add keywords, search themes, or audiences!
